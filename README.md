In the world of Python, a `.whl` (Wheel) is a built package—ready to run, ready to serve. In my world, `Zen-Whl` is the action of taking the chaotic, infinite universe and building something structured for humanity.

* **Zen (The Void):** The infinite, untouched universe. Without human consciousness to observe it, it contains everything yet means nothing.
* **Whl (The Packaged Wheel):** My personal wheel. It's the deliberate choice to extract meaning, package logic, and keep humanity spinning.
